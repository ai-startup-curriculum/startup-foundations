# Prerequisites — Startup Foundations track

**Role level:** 10 (foundational shared root — Startup family)
**Track:** `startup-foundations`

This track is the **root** of the ai-startup-curriculum org — the shared foundation every functional pillar and role pathway inherits at 100%. It assumes almost nothing about prior startup experience and everything about willingness to think in systems. The five modules move a learner from a definition of what a startup is, through the dependency graph, the stage axis, and the founder's core operating loop, to the three founder-numbers. Depth in each pillar lives in the pillar's own repo — Foundations does not re-teach it.

## Assumed lower-level curriculum

None. Startup Foundations is the entry point to the org. If you have never worked at or started a startup before, this is the correct place to start.

If you have already been operating as a founder for a while, you may still find Foundations useful as a *reset* — it names the pieces of the mental model that most founders assemble incidentally through pattern-matching, and the exercises are designed to be run against a *real* startup you already know.

## Assumed working knowledge

Even without prior startup experience, you should be practically comfortable with:

- **Reading long-form essays and marketing / investor writing critically** — you should be able to open a Paul Graham essay, a Sequoia narrative, or a First Round Review piece and pull the underlying argument out without being carried by the prose.
- **Basic spreadsheet fluency** — enough to build a working runway model in mod-005: rows of monthly cash-in and cash-out, running balance, a sensitivity-scenario column. Google Sheets, Excel, or Numbers are all fine.
- **Basic vocabulary of money and time** — revenue vs. profit, monthly cadence vs. quarterly, fixed vs. variable cost, gross vs. net. If these are unfamiliar, work through the [Corporate Finance Institute — Startup Finance](https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/knowledge/finance/startup/) primer once before starting mod-005.
- **Ability to write a one-page memo** — mod-004 and every project ship written artifacts (a founder-narrative in the Sequoia shape, a decision log, an investor update, a two-slide diagnostic). If you have never written a business memo, treat the mod-001 → mod-004 exercises as writing practice as well as content practice.
- **Willingness to work against a real (or realistic hypothetical) startup** — every module has exercises and every project has a deliverable that only becomes concrete when applied to a specific company. Choose one — yours if you have one, or a well-documented public one — before starting mod-002.

## Not assumed, taught here

You do **not** need prior experience with:

- Any specific founder-facing framework — the Steve Blank customer-development model, Eric Ries's build-measure-learn loop, the Lean Canvas, the Business Model Canvas, or the Sequoia narrative shape. Mod-001 and mod-002 cover each at the working level.
- Any specific stage vocabulary — IDEA / PRE-SEED / SEED / SERIES-A / GROWTH / MATURE. Mod-003 covers the stage ladder, the signals, and the stage-appropriate focus.
- Any specific founder-operating rhythm — Monday plans, Friday reviews, decision logs, lightweight OKRs, investor-update shapes. Mod-004 walks the loop end-to-end.
- Any specific founder-numbers vocabulary — runway, burn (gross / net / after-variable-revenue), growth (WoW / MoM), 'default alive or default dead', CAC / LTV / payback / gross margin, North-Star metric. Mod-005 covers each with runnable spreadsheet drills.
- Any specific investor / VC vocabulary — SAFE, term sheet, cap table, option pool, dilution, board control. Mod-002 places each on the corporate-structure branch of the graph and routes the depth to `startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum` and `startup-operations-governance-curriculum`.

## Recommended reading before starting

- [Paul Graham — 'How to Start a Startup'](https://paulgraham.com/start.html) — read once to set expectations for the founder mindset.
- [Paul Graham — 'Startup = Growth'](https://paulgraham.com/growth.html) — the operational definition of a startup by its growth rate; load-bearing in mod-001 and mod-005.
- [Paul Graham — 'Default Alive or Default Dead'](https://paulgraham.com/aord.html) — the composite founder question; load-bearing in mod-005.
- [Steve Blank — *The Four Steps to the Epiphany* (Stanford handout PDF)](https://web.stanford.edu/group/e145/cgi-bin/spring/upload/handouts/Four_Steps.pdf) — read the introduction and the customer-development overview; mod-001 and mod-002 refer to it directly.
- [Eric Ries — *The Lean Startup* principles page](http://theleanstartup.com/principles) — read the principles once; mod-001, mod-002, and mod-004 build on the vocabulary.
- [Sequoia Capital — Writing a Business Plan](https://articles.sequoiacap.com/writing-a-business-plan) — read once as the shape the mod-004 founder-narrative is authored against.
- One recent [CB Insights startup post-mortem](https://www.cbinsights.com/research/startup-failure-reasons-top/) or a [Founder Collective 'mortema'](https://foundercollective.com/mortemas/) — read one end-to-end so mod-001 exercise-05 (three failure-post-mortem teardown) has a running start.

## Not required, useful to have

- **Prior founder or founding-team exposure** — a first-hire seat at a pre-Series-A startup, an accelerator batch experience, a founder-in-residence rotation, or a chief-of-staff-to-founder role. Foundations does not require it but the exercises are richer against a real context.
- **Familiarity with a Business Model Canvas or Lean Canvas** — mod-002 exercise-05 compares the two; prior exposure makes the compare drill faster.
- **A running spreadsheet you already keep for your own numbers** — mod-005 lab-01 becomes an incremental upgrade rather than a from-zero build.

Foundations covers the founder-numbers slice (runway, burn, growth); depth lives there.

- **Exit and endgame (M&A, secondaries, IPO, succession, shutdown) *depth*** — owned by [`startup-exit-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-exit-curriculum) (level 40); actionable only at MATURE.
- **Role-specific curated views** — [`founder-ceo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/founder-ceo-curriculum) (level 30), [`cto-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cto-curriculum) (level 35), and [`cpo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cpo-curriculum) (level 30) — inherit Foundations at 100% and add role-specific pillar coverage.
- **Legal opinion** — out of scope; route to counsel.
- **Personal tax / immigration / accounting** — out of scope; route to a qualified professional.
