# Startup Foundations Curriculum

The shared root every role pathway starts from (100% coverage for all roles).
Foundations teaches the *system* and the *graph* — the mental model that makes
every downstream pillar legible.

## Modules

| Module | Stage | Pillar | What you'll be able to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mod-001-how-startups-work` | IDEA | foundations | Explain a startup as a system: the search for a repeatable, scalable business model under uncertainty |
| `mod-002-the-dependency-graph` | IDEA | foundations | Place any "business thing" on the graph and know what it depends on and when it matters |
| `mod-003-stages-and-what-matters-when` | IDEA | foundations | Map activities to stages; know what to ignore at your stage |
| `mod-004-founder-operating-basics` | PRE-SEED | foundations | Run the founder's core loop: decide, allocate, communicate, measure |
| `mod-005-reading-the-numbers` | PRE-SEED | economics | Read the three founder numbers — runway, burn, growth — and what they imply |

> **Status:** taxonomy authored (`STARTUP_STAGES.md`, `FUNCTIONAL_CURRICULA.md`).
> Module lessons are filled by the autonomous research→author pipeline, oldest-gap
> first. The graph and stage docs are the source of truth and are human-maintained.

## How this repo relates to the others

Foundations defines the axes; the role repos apply them. Start here, then follow
your **role pathway** (see [FUNCTIONAL_CURRICULA.md](./FUNCTIONAL_CURRICULA.md))
through the functional pillars in dependency order.
